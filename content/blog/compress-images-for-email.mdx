Email portals and mail apps often reject large attachments. A few clear steps usually fix the problem without turning your photo into a blurry mess.

## Why email needs smaller images

Many providers limit attachments to a few megabytes. Even when a file is accepted, large photos slow sending and download time for the person on the other end.

## A simple workflow

1. Crop out unused background
2. Resize to the width you actually need (often 1200–1600px for photos)
3. Compress with a Balanced or High Quality preset
4. Preview faces, text and product details
5. Attach and send

If a portal asks for under 100KB or 200KB, use CompressKit’s target-size tools and review the result before uploading.

## Format tips for email

- **Photos:** JPG or WebP
- **Screenshots with text:** PNG or WebP
- **Graphics with transparency:** PNG

## Common mistakes

- Compressing a huge camera original without resizing first
- Dropping quality too far before checking the preview
- Sending PNG photos when JPG would be much smaller
